How It Works

1 Tap the paperclip button (right side, next to queue) to open the attachment modal.
2 Badge shows count of attached files. Visible when files are attached, hidden when empty.
3 In the modal, you can rename files (tap pencil icon), remove files (tap X), or add more.
4 Tap Done or outside the modal to close. Files stay attached until you send or remove them.
